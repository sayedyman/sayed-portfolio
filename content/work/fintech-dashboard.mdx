## The Business Problem

Apex Financial was losing enterprise clients because their core dashboard was too slow, visually outdated, and difficult to navigate. Users couldn't find the data they needed quickly, leading to high support ticket volumes.

## The Approach

We started by interviewing 15 power users to map out their daily workflows. We discovered that 80% of their time was spent on just three core screens.

### Key Decisions
1. **Prioritize Data Density**: Enterprise users needed to see more data at a glance, without feeling overwhelmed.
2. **Implement a Strong Typography Hierarchy**: Using Inter for numbers and Clash Display for headers to ensure maximum legibility for financial data.
3. **Dark Mode First**: Traders preferred dark interfaces to reduce eye strain during long sessions.

## The Outcome

The redesigned dashboard reduced the average task completion time by 40% and decreased support tickets related to navigation by 60% within the first month of launch.
