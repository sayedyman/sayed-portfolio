{/* ── ACT I — CONTEXT ──────────────────────────────────────────── */}

<CaseStudyProse>
  Apex Financial was losing enterprise clients. Not because the product failed to function — it did. But function without clarity is friction. Their core dashboard had grown into something their own users no longer trusted to read correctly.

  High support ticket volumes. Long onboarding periods. Power users building workarounds in spreadsheets. The interface communicated data, but it did not communicate meaning.
</CaseStudyProse>

<CaseStudySilent type="line" text="The interface was only part of the problem." />

{/* ── ACT II — THE WORK ────────────────────────────────────────── */}

<CaseStudyPullquote
  text="The real problem wasn't the interface. It was the mental model."
  alignment="left"
/>

<CaseStudyMicroText>What happened next changed the direction of the entire project.</CaseStudyMicroText>

<CaseStudyProse>
  Fifteen interviews with power users revealed a consistent pattern: 80% of their daily time was spent across just three screens. Everything else was noise. The redesign had a clear mandate — make those three screens exceptional, and let the rest follow.
</CaseStudyProse>

<CaseStudySilent type="space" />

<CaseStudyDecisionGroup>
  <CaseStudyDecision
    label="Prioritize the three core screens"
    rationale="Rather than redesigning the entire product at once, we restructured the navigation to surface these three screens as first-class destinations. Every other feature became secondary, accessible but not competing for attention."
  />
  <CaseStudyDecision
    label="Implement a strong typography hierarchy"
    rationale="Financial data lives and dies by legibility. Inter was chosen for numerical data — its tabular figures align cleanly in dense tables. Clash Display was reserved for headers, creating a clear visual register between data and context."
  />
  <CaseStudyDecision
    label="Dark mode as the primary experience"
    rationale="Traders and analysts work long sessions under artificial light. A dark interface reduces eye strain and, critically, increases the perceived contrast of critical numbers — the data reads first, the chrome recedes."
  />
</CaseStudyDecisionGroup>

<CaseStudySilent type="space" />

{/* ── ACT III — RESOLUTION ─────────────────────────────────────── */}

<CaseStudyOutcome metrics={outcomeMetrics} />

<CaseStudySilent type="space" />

<CaseStudyReflection>
  The most impactful decision was the one we almost didn't make: reducing scope. There was pressure to redesign everything. Doing so would have produced a more impressive demo and a less useful product.

  What shipped felt smaller than what we imagined. It worked better than anything we had imagined.
</CaseStudyReflection>
